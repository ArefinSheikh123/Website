The Woodhouse Experience

Crafted by Flame, Defined by Uncompromising Quality

Nestled at Bay's 23 on Gulshan Avenue, Woodhouse Grill Gulshan was born from a singular obsession: bringing authentic, old-world wood-fired steakhouse mastery to Dhaka's discerning diners.

Every cut in our curing room is hand-selected for superior intramuscular marbling, seasoned with coarse artisanal sea salt and cracked Kampot peppercorn, and seared over seasoned hardwood embers to lock in deep, smoky complexity. Whether you are hosting an executive dinner, celebrating an anniversary, or gathering with friends over a carved Tomahawk, our warm timber-clad sanctuary sets the stage.

Artisanal Cuts

100% Halal-certified prime Australian, Argentine-style, and local artisanal beef cuts, carefully aged for 28 to 45 days to achieve butter-knife tenderness and rich umami depth.

Wood-Fired Perfection

Custom-built open-flame grates fueled by seasoned hardwood and lump charcoal sear at 450°C, basted with roasted bone marrow and smoked herb butter right before resting.

Premier Gulshan Dining

Deep walnut interiors, warm amber lighting, acoustic privacy, and white-glove hospitality in the diplomatic and commercial heart of Gulshan-1 & Gulshan Avenue.
